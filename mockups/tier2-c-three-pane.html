Fuel exhaustion-related forced landings in Canadian aviation typically result from inadequate pre-flight planning, misjudgment of fuel reserves, or unrecognized fuel-flow issues12.

TSB investigation A13Q0098 found that the pilot departed without verifying actual fuel quantity and relied on time-based estimates1. Similar findings appear in A08C0124 and A03A0013, where pilots did not switch tanks at appropriate intervals23.

Recommendation: brief pilots on the difference between fuel exhaustion and fuel starvation, and reinforce pre-flight dipstick verification per AC 605-0024.
