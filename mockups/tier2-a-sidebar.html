Fuel exhaustion-related forced landings in Canadian aviation typically result from inadequate pre-flight planning, misjudgment of fuel reserves, or unrecognized fuel-flow issues. TSB investigation A13Q0098 ("Forced landing following fuel exhaustion") found that the pilot departed without verifying actual fuel quantity and relied on time-based estimates. Similar findings appear in A08C0124 and A03A0013 (fuel starvation / forced landing), where pilots did not switch tanks at appropriate intervals. Recommendation: brief pilots on the difference between fuel exhaustion and fuel starvation, and reinforce pre-flight dipstick verification per AC 605-002.
